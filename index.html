About Organ Donation

What is Organ Donation?

Organ donation is the process of surgically removing an organ or tissue from one person (the organ donor) and placing it into another person (the recipient).

Why It Matters in Uganda

Many patients in Uganda suffer from organ failure. Raising awareness and increasing donor registration can save countless lives.

Who Can Donate

Can I Donate?

Most people can be considered as potential donors. Age and medical history will be evaluated at the time of donation.

Myths & Facts

  • Myth: It is against my culture.
    Fact: Many cultures view donation as an act of charity.
  • Myth: It costs money.
    Fact: The donor's family is never charged for donation.

Religious Views

Most major religions, including Christianity and Islam, support organ donation as an act of saving life.

Living vs Deceased Donation

Living: A living person can donate a kidney or part of their liver.
Deceased: Organs are donated after a person has passed away.
